SEO for financial advisers:
trust, compliance
and leads.

Finance buyers research carefully and compare many firms. SEO helps you show up, but trust and compliance decide who they contact.

By Santiago Payá de la Cruz•October 16, 2026•8 min read

How finance buyers search

They search for services, comparisons and local advisers: 'independent financial adviser [city]', 'wealth management for entrepreneurs', 'retirement planning advice'. They read widely before contacting anyone.

Your content should answer their questions without hype.

Service pages with clarity

One page per service, written plainly: who it is for, how you work, how you are paid, how the process runs and what to prepare. Transparency about fees builds trust.

Avoid promises of returns.

Compliance first

Financial promotion rules vary by country. Have content reviewed by compliance before publication, include required disclosures and avoid misleading claims.

Good SEO and good compliance are compatible when the writing is honest.

Trust signals

Qualifications, regulatory registration, team profiles, client types served and independent reviews help. Put them near contact forms.

Make it easy to verify who you are.

Educational content

Guides on pensions, tax-efficient saving or succession planning attract searchers early in their journey. They must be accurate, dated and reviewed.

Link them to the relevant service pages.

Measurement

Track enquiries, booked calls and qualified leads, not only traffic. Review which pages contribute and which topics deserve more depth.

Report in plain language.

Where it usually goes wrong

The most frequent errors:

  • Promising returns.
  • Skipping compliance review.
  • Thin service pages.
  • Outdated guides.
  • Measuring only visits.

Compliance first, always

Anything you publish must fit your regulator's rules on claims and risk warnings. I write and structure content, but your compliance officer signs it off.

Within those limits, clarity wins: who you advise, how fees work, what the first meeting looks like.
