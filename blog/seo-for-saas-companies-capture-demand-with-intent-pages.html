SEO for SaaS:
capture demand
with intent pages.

The best SaaS SEO starts where buyers already look for a solution. Intent pages beat generic blog volume almost every time.

By Santiago Payá de la Cruz•October 18, 2026•8 min read

Start with buyer intent

Buyers search for a problem, a use case, a competitor or an alternative. Pages built for those searches convert far better than general articles.

Map your keywords by intent before writing anything.

Use-case and industry pages

One page per main use case or industry: the problem, how the product solves it, proof and a clear next step. Use real examples, not generic claims.

Each page should stand alone.

Comparison and alternative pages

'X vs Y' and 'alternatives to X' pages capture buyers late in the funnel. Be fair and factual. Honest comparisons build trust and are cited more often by AI assistants.

Update them regularly.

Pricing and clarity

A clear pricing page answers a very common search and filters leads. If pricing is custom, explain what affects it.

Avoid hiding everything behind a form.

Technical foundations

Fast pages, clean structure, crawlable documentation and structured data support everything else. Make sure public docs and integration pages are indexed.

Check rendering if the site relies on heavy JavaScript.

Measurement

Track demo requests, trial sign-ups and pipeline from organic, not only visits. Review which intent pages contribute and expand those.

Align reporting with sales.

Where it usually goes wrong

The most frequent errors:

  • Writing broad blog posts first.
  • Hiding pricing.
  • Biased comparisons.
  • Ignoring documentation SEO.
  • Reporting traffic only.

Where I would start

Use-case pages, comparison and alternative pages, and a clear pricing page. These capture people already close to buying.

The blog comes later. Write it once the money pages exist, and link it to them.
