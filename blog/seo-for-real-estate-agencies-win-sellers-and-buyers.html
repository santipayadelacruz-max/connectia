Portals take most property searches. An agency wins where they cannot: neighbourhood knowledge, trust and winning sellers.

By Santiago Payá de la Cruz•October 22, 2026•8 min read

Where an agency can compete

Competing with big portals for 'houses for sale' is hard. You can win local searches by neighbourhood, seller-intent searches and service searches such as valuations or lettings.

Your edge is local knowledge a portal cannot copy.

Neighbourhood pages

Write useful pages per area: typical prices, property types, schools, transport, local life and real examples. Pages that only swap the area name add nothing.

Each page must answer what a buyer or seller in that area wants to know.

Winning sellers

Searches like 'sell my house in [area]' or 'how much is my home worth' are very valuable. A clear valuation page with a short form and proof of results captures them.

Show how you work, realistic selling times and client reviews.

Listing pages

Each listing on your site needs a unique description, optimised photos and structured data. When a property sells, redirect or convert the page into a case rather than deleting it.

Avoid copying portal descriptions.

Google Business Profile and reviews

The profile matters for local-intent searches. Keep categories, photos and hours accurate and respond to every review. Ask for feedback after each completed deal.

Link to valuation and area pages.

Measurement

Measure calls, valuation requests and enquiries per listing, not only visits. Review monthly which areas bring the most contacts.

Use it to guide content.

Where it usually goes wrong

The most frequent errors:

  • Duplicate area pages.
  • Total reliance on portals.
  • Neglected profile.
  • Not tracking contacts.
  • Deleting sold listings.

Where I would put the effort

Neighbourhood pages with real information (typical prices, schools, transport) and a clear page for owners who want to sell or let. That is where business starts.

And a Google profile with reviews. An owner choosing between three agencies reads the reviews before calling.
