Quick answer

How do you get more enquiries from Google in your business?

Local SEO in your business means your business appears in Google, Maps and AI assistants when people nearby search for what you offer. It works on three fronts (your Google profile, a website with one page per service, and reviews) and is measured in calls, directions and forms, not visits.

  • Who it is forBusinesses in your business and the surrounding area, especially restaurants, clinics, shops and services.
  • What is includedGoogle profile, website SEO, reviews, AI search visibility and a monthly report.
  • TimingFirst improvements in weeks; in competitive markets, 3 to 6 months. No guaranteed rankings.
  • PriceFixed and in writing after a free 30-minute call.

How Google decides

Google's three local factors

Google describes them as relevance, distance and prominence. Two you can shape; one is earned.

Relevance

How well your profile matches what the person searches.

What we do Clear categories, services and content.

Distance

How close you are to the searcher or the area they name.

What we do Correct service area and details; you cannot move, but you can make the most of it.

Prominence

How well known and well rated you are, on the web and beyond.

What we do Reviews, mentions, links and a solid website.
