What does not fit a short timeline

Some work cannot be rushed without hurting quality: long copywriting from scratch, professional photo or video shoots, complex integrations with other systems and large professional translations. They can be done, but are best agreed separately with their own timeline and budget so the launch is not delayed.

The rule is simple: the essentials launch first, done well, and the rest is added calmly afterwards.
