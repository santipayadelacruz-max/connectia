What is included

Everything needed to launch
and start winning clients

Custom, mobile-first design

Design built for phones first, where most visits happen, then adapted to tablet and desktop. No recognisable template look: colours, type and layout follow your brand and the action you want visitors to take.

SEO foundations from day one

Unique titles and descriptions per page, clean heading structure and URLs, structured data and a sitemap submitted to Google. It is the base that lets the site rank, not an add-on.

Speed and technical quality

Optimised images, lightweight code and real mobile speed checks before launch. A slow site loses visitors and rankings, so speed is part of the design.

Contact form and clear calls to action

Short form, visible contact buttons and a clear contact page. Every enquiry reaches your inbox and is measurable.

Analytics and Search Console

Visit and enquiry tracking from the first day, plus Search Console so you can see how people find you on Google.

Handover with an editing guide

A short guide to change text, images and prices yourself, and all accounts in your name: domain, hosting and dashboard.

What does not fit a short timeline

Some work cannot be rushed without hurting quality: long copywriting from scratch, professional photo or video shoots, complex integrations with other systems and large professional translations. They can be done, but are best agreed separately with their own timeline and budget so the launch is not delayed.

The rule is simple: the essentials launch first, done well, and the rest is added calmly afterwards.
