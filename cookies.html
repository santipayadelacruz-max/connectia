Cookie policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser to remember information such as preferences or session data. Some are essential for a site to work; others measure visits or show advertising.

Which cookies this site uses

connectia.one does not currently use first-party or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not store data in your browser's local storage. That is why there is no consent banner: there is nothing to accept.

If an analytics tool or other technology that needs non-essential cookies is added in future, your consent will be requested before it is switched on and this page will be updated.
